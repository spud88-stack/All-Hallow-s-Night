    Ways out

    • Drive. Find the car keys hidden somewhere in the neighborhood and reach the car. If he's tampered with it, a toolkit can fix it.
    • Call the police. Use a landline with a working line, or a charged cell phone. Survive until they arrive, then get to a cruiser with an officer nearby. More calls, gunfire and bodies make the response escalate.
    • Survive until dawn (5:00 AM).

    Fear

    Darkness, bodies, screams, and above all him raise your fear. High fear makes your hands shake, drains stamina, narrows your vision, and makes you audible while hiding. The killer feeds on it and grows stronger. Light, allies, candy and hiding calm you down.

    The killers

    Freddy only exists in your dreams: stay awake, and if you fall asleep, find an alarm clock. Leatherface charges with a chainsaw that stalls after each charge, and the corn hides you. Chucky freezes like a toy whenever you watch him.

    The Shape watches you from the dark until you're broken. Jason is relentless and appears where you least expect. Ghostface is fast, calls you on the phone, and hides until he's close. Hold your flashlight on him to expose him.
